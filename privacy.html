Privacy Policy

Effective 14 November 2026 · Operator: marpfirst (individual creator, Indonesia)

Studio Konten ("the app") is a private tool that marpfirst uses to publish videos and reply to comments on accounts that marpfirst owns or manages. This policy explains what data the app accesses and what happens to it.

Data the app accesses

  • YouTube (Google OAuth, scope youtube.force-ssl): the connected channel's ID and name; videos uploaded through the app and their status; public comments on the channel's own videos (author display name, comment text, time, like count); replies the owner sends.
  • Instagram and Facebook (Meta): the connected Instagram professional account and Facebook Page ID and name; media published through the app; comments on the owner's own posts; replies the owner sends.
  • Files: the video and thumbnail files the owner chooses to publish.

The app does not access viewers' private data, email addresses, messages, contacts, or any account that the owner does not control.

How the data is used

  • To upload, schedule and verify the owner's videos.
  • To show comments on the owner's videos and prepare draft replies. Comment text may be sent to an AI model provider (currently Anthropic) only to write a draft; the owner reviews every draft before anything is sent.
  • Data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else, except the platforms themselves when publishing and the AI provider for drafts as described above.

Google API Services

Studio Konten's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; by connecting a YouTube account you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

Storage and security

Data is stored in a private database operated by marpfirst. Access tokens are encrypted (AES-256-GCM). Files are kept in private storage that is not publicly reachable.

Retention and deletion

  • Comments and drafts are kept only as long as they are needed to reply, and are refreshed from the platform when the app runs.
  • When an account is disconnected, its tokens and stored data are deleted within 30 days.
  • You can revoke access at any time: Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions; Meta in Instagram or Facebook settings under "Apps and websites".
  • To ask for deletion of any data about you (for example, a comment you wrote), email the address below. Requests are handled within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date.
