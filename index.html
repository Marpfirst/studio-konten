Studio Konten

A private publishing tool operated by marpfirst, an independent creator in Indonesia.

Studio Konten helps marpfirst publish the same short video to the creator's own accounts and answer viewer comments. It is used only for accounts that marpfirst owns or manages, including the YouTube channel and Instagram account "Tebak Siapa Dia?".

What it does

  • Uploads a finished video to YouTube as Private, sets the title, description, tags and thumbnail, and schedules the time it becomes Public.
  • Publishes the same video as an Instagram Reel and to a Facebook Page at a time the owner approves.
  • Reads comments on the owner's own videos, prepares draft replies, and sends a reply only after the owner checks and approves it.

How access works

The owner connects each account with the platform's own sign-in screen (Google OAuth for YouTube, Meta login for Instagram and Facebook). Access can be removed at any time from the platform's security settings. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
